Understanding RFID Scanner (Reader)

An RFID scanner (or reader) is a device that reads the information on RFID tags, or small labels attached to objects that consist of a microchip and an antenna. RFID does not need a line-of-sight, like barcodes: multiple tags can be detected at once, and even when not directly seen by the scanners. It enables quick, precise identification of items, inventory, access control, etc., transforming the efficiency of modern businesses.

Our Range of RFID Scanner Models

As an experienced RFID scanner suppliers in Pune, we provide different range of scanners-

  1. Handheld RFID Scanners- Our RFID scanner reader dealers in Pune deals with portable readers that scan RFID tags in warehouses, retail stores, and logistics. They are mobile and flexible to enable inventory, asset tracking, and field operations scanning on the go.
  2. Fixed RFID Readers- Stationary Concrete devices that are set at certain points such as entry/exit points, conveyor belt, or counter. They read RFID tags without interference, thus they could be continuously and reliably tracked.
  3. Integrated RFID Modules- RFID readers that are embedded in other systems or devices. These modules offer smooth RFID operation with existing equipment and allow efficient tracking and automation without additional hardware.
  4. Sled RFID Readers- Mobility-based devices, which are attached to smartphones or tablets and convert them into mobile RFID scanners. In those applications that need portability and the ability to use the existing mobile devices to capture data.
  5. UHF RFID Readers- Readers in the Ultra High Frequency (UHF) band are long-range scans. Widely applicable in inventory control, warehouse processes, and tracking assets that require warehouses to read tags at a distance.
