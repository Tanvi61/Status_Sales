GoDEX G500 Barcode Printer Series User Manual

High-Tech Power with a Customer Friendly Price


The GoDEX G500 Barcode Printer Series is engineered for high-efficiency, long-lasting, and high-performance barcode printing across a variety of industrial and commercial environments. This series of printers is designed for ease of use and supports either direct thermal or thermal transfer printing to meet different labeling requirements. With an advanced mechanism and strong motor, the G500 can print large volumes of labels reliably. The printer is capable of quick print speeds and high-quality or high-density printing across a large range of labels - making this a very suitable option for retail, logistics, warehouse or manufacturing duty cycle requirements. With easy ribbon and labels, USB and serial connections and support for various operating systems and software platforms, it is easily adjustable for any user's workflow integration.
